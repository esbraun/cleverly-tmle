# The clustered interval floor of cross-fitted LTMLE

This study sets the clustered interval floor of cross-fitted `LTMLE`: the smallest positive-mass
cluster count from which its interval on $t_{J-2}$ carries no experimental label
([few clusters](../inference.md#few-clusters)). It measures every count from 4 to 20 clusters,
and 30 and 39, and reads the floor by the rule declared before the run. On the same draws it
compares the shipped reference (A) with Wang et al.'s inflated variance (B) and the
delete-one-cluster jackknife (C).

No other implementation computes this interval at these counts, so the study has no comparator. The
publication policy is `reporting`. Every count passes, so the family's floor is 4.

Every property cell reads one contrast, `ate_regimen[always vs never]`, on two binary treatment
nodes with censoring. `CLUSTER_INTERVAL_FLOORS` ships the floor of 4 for that contrast on that
design alone. Every other estimate, the second contrast included, keeps 20, and so does the
contrast on any other design. No row is red, so the study holds no row in the [red-cell ledger](red-cells.md).

| comparator runs | none | no other implementation computes this interval |

## What was fitted

The law is the end-of-study law of `few-cluster-cross-fitted-ltmle` with a scaled cluster latent,
at `equal40` (clusters of 40 rows) and `unequal40` (sizes uniform on 10 to 70). Each fit is the
`canonical-ltmle-crossfit` subject: $V = \min(5, J)$ whole-cluster folds, the law's own treatment
and censoring mechanisms, and a quasibinomial GLM for every node regression, run with
`numerical_separation=True`. The jackknife (C) runs where every fold holds at least 2 clusters,
from 10 clusters. The law, ladder and seeds are in `tests/studies/few_cluster_floor_ltmle.py`,
and `tests/unit/test_few_cluster_floor_design.py` recomputes them.

## Limitations

- **One measured contrast.** Every property cell reads always against never. Every other estimate
  keeps the floor of 20.
- **Parametric learners.** Every node regression is a quasibinomial GLM and the mechanisms are the
  law's own. Wang et al. (2024), Remark 3, caution against flexible learners at about 20 clusters.
  [`few-cluster-adaptive-learners`](few-cluster-adaptive-learners.md) measures the default learner
  library at 20 clusters.
- **One law.** The end-of-study law at two size laws, with censoring and no weights.
- **No comparator.** No other implementation computes this interval at these counts.
