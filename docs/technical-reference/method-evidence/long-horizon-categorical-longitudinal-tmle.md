# Long-horizon categorical longitudinal TMLE

This study validates `LTMLE` with three-level treatment nodes at three, four and five decisions, in sample and
cross-fitted over five folds. The laws come from `tests/discrete_law_longitudinal_horizon.py`, which gives the
exact truth of every estimand. The comparator is R
[`lmtp`](https://www.jstatsoft.org/article/view/v102i09) at commit `9996b04` (version 1.5.5), fitted on the
first 100 replications of each paired scenario. On the four in-sample cells of the stochastic plan `mix`,
its estimate and standard error agree with those of `cleverly` to within $10^{-6}$ on every paired
replication. The other paired rows are descriptive.

The study publishes under the `reporting` policy. The design lists 40 gated truth rows and 17 gated
calibration cells before any run (`tests/long_horizon_design.py`). A red gated row or cell fails
`tests/unit/test_long_horizon_design.py`. A row outside the list reports its measured value and blocks nothing. The
support rows `G46.tmle.categorical_*` in `src/cleverly/_support_rows.py` cite this study.

## What was compared

Each scenario draws 2,000 replications at the $n$ in the table. A scenario whose rows fail the exact-law
criterion on the base law $L_R$ splits by law. Each fallback law that a row needs gets one companion scenario,
which fits the reference and the plans of the rows it gates. A law is written $(B, s, c_0)$.

| scenario | law | $n$ | plans |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `categorical_k3` | $L_R$ | 28,000 | `low`, `high`, `respond`, `switch`, `mix`, `mixlast` |
| `categorical_k4` | $L_R$ | 128,000 | `low`, `respond`, `switch`, `mix`, `mixlast` |
| `categorical_k4_high` | (0.75, 0.1, 3.5) | 108,000 | `low`, `high` |
| `categorical_k4_static` | (1, 0, 4.5) | 124,000 | `low`, `high` |
| `categorical_k5` | $L_R$ | 116,000 | `low`, `respond`, `mix`, `mixlast` |
| `categorical_k5_static` | (0.5, 0.05, 3.5) | 124,000 | `low`, `high`, `switch` |
| `categorical_k5_switch` | (0.5, 0.1, 3.5) | 72,000 | `low`, `switch` |
| `categorical_k5_high` | (0.25, 0, 3.5) | 64,000 | `low`, `high` |
| `categorical_k5_mix` | (1, 0.05, 3.5) | 96,000 | `low`, `mix` |
| `categorical_k5_crossfit` | $L_R$ | 116,000 | the plans of `categorical_k5`, at five folds on its realized sample |

The policy scenarios also report the paired contrast of `mix` against `mixlast`. The two plans share every
follower path. `tests/longitudinal_horizon_plans.py` states each plan as the regimen that `LTMLE` takes.

| setting | `cleverly` | R `lmtp` |
| --- | --- | --- |
| mechanism | the law's treatment and censoring probabilities, passed as probability columns | the per-node density ratios computed from the same probabilities (`tests/studies/long_horizon_reference.py`) |
| bounds | `g_bounds=(1e-8, 1)` | the supplied ratios, computed from the same bounded probabilities |
| outcome fit | the declared quasibinomial GLM for each outcome and pseudo-outcome regression | the same quasibinomial fit on the units that follow the plan, supplied to `lmtp` as a learner (`tests/canonical/lmtp_horizon_learners.R`) |
| cross-fitting | five folds in `categorical_k5_crossfit`, with the pooled update | not paired. The pinned `lmtp` fits a separate fluctuation in each fold and runs no pooled update, so it has no counterpart to this scenario |
| intervals | Wald for every row. A single-fold scenario also writes a BCa row from 199 bootstrap refits | Wald from the influence curve. No bootstrap |
| replications | 2,000 per scenario | the first 100 of each paired scenario, on the same draws |
| unpaired plans | fits every plan | not paired: `switch` in `categorical_k4`, `high` in `categorical_k4_high`, `high` and `switch` in `categorical_k5_static`, `switch` in `categorical_k5_switch` and `high` in `categorical_k5_high`, with every contrast that reads one. The pinned `lmtp` fits its node-$K$ fluctuation by `glm` at R's default start, which diverges under cumulative weights near $10^3$ and reports $\varepsilon \approx 3 \times 10^{15}$. On replicates 0 to 99 the default-start fit differs from the rescaled-weight fit by more than $10^{-6}$ in 100, 100, 100, 100, 70 and 10 replicates of the cells listed in the order above (`high` before `switch` in `categorical_k5_static`), and in none for the other plans. Node $K$ only. A run of the whole `lmtp` one-fold loop on replicates 0 to 99 of every paired plan found no diverged node (largest $\lvert\varepsilon\rvert$ 0.45; `tests/canonical/lmtp_9996b04/emulation-summary.csv`). A replicate whose fit reports a non-finite $\varepsilon$ or one above $10^3$ at any node is recorded as a failure, and the run recorded none. Source: `tests/canonical/lmtp_9996b04/fluctuation_scan.py` |

## Limitations

| limit | what it means for use |
| --- | --- |
| one law family with three-level nodes | the laws are the $(B, s, c_0)$ family of `tests/discrete_law_longitudinal_horizon.py`. The study does not cover more than three arms, continuous covariates or a continuous outcome |
| the exact comparison covers one plan | R `lmtp` 9996b04 matches the estimate and standard error of `mix` in four scenarios on 100 replications. The other paired cells agree to $3 \times 10^{-9}$ but carry no verdict. The R truth rows cannot resolve the declared margins at 100 replications, and the margins rows state no verdict |
| six plans and the cross-fitted scenario are unpaired | the pinned `lmtp` fluctuation diverges on the six plans, and it has no pooled cross-fitted update. These rows have no external check |
| the declared mechanism is exact | the law's probabilities enter as columns, so the study measures the targeting and outcome regressions with the mechanism known. The `learned_mechanism` row reports one learned case and gates nothing |
| the outcome regressions are one quasibinomial GLM | the exact-law criterion reads the declared GLM curve. The cross-fitted cells draw grown trees only in `crossfit_overfitting` |
| the Wald interval gates only at $n \ge 2 n_\kappa$ | below that size the Wald row reports and the BCa row gates. A reader who needs a valid interval at a smaller $n$ must read the BCa row |
| the cross-fitted scenario reports Wald rows only | the cross-fitted bootstrap is experimental (X20). The cross-fitted rows gate through `interval_calibration` cells at $2 n_\kappa$ |
| support loss is reported and not gated | the `support_loss` rows report the binding share per node. The study states no rule for how much support loss an estimate tolerates |
| the bootstrap band gates at one plan set | the band covers the six $K$ = 5 plans on one law at $n$ = 64,000. The study does not cover a band over another plan set or law |

The causal interpretation requires consistency, sequential exchangeability, longitudinal positivity, and
conditionally independent censoring. The calibration, bias and rate families test the validity of the interval
and the estimate under those conditions. They do not test the conditions.
