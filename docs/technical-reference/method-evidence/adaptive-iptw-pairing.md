# Adaptive-propensity IPTW against drtmle

This study pairs `AdaptiveIPTW` with R [`drtmle`](https://github.com/benkeser/drtmle) 1.1.2 at
commit `538a3a2`, function `adaptive_iptw`, replicate by replicate. Both sides see the same rows,
the same folds and the same initial mechanism at each key. Both fit the reduced regression $Q_r$
on the same basis of $\operatorname{logit} g$ and target the propensity along $Q_r / g$. The
[gate study](adaptive-iptw.md) holds the property evidence for the method, and the
[contract page](../adaptive-iptw.md) states the estimator and its conditions.

The verdict is per-replicate agreement. On each key the 99th percentile of
$|\hat\psi_{\text{cleverly}} - \hat\psi_{\text{drtmle}}| / \widehat{\mathrm{SE}}$ over the
replicates both sides returned must stay under a bound declared before the run. That rule sees
a dropped or misplaced targeting step that a mean difference would average away.

## What was compared

| setting | `cleverly` | R `drtmle` |
| --- | --- | --- |
| datasets | 1,000 samples for each scenario, of 2,000 rows (3,000 on laws P, RM and ST), drawn from this study's own seeds. Scenarios on one law and fold form share their samples | the identical rows |
| laws | the gate study's laws P, PD, M, C (binary and three-arm), CN, RM, MA and ST. See the [law table](adaptive-iptw.md#what-was-compared) | the same rows. The runner builds each regime, composite and natural-course recode from the raw columns |
| mechanism | the gate study's learner at each key: the declared known mechanism on PD and ST, an estimated one elsewhere | `gn`, composed in R from this package's raw fitted factors and checked against the package's mechanism to $10^{-12}$ |
| folds | 10 folds on the cross-fitted scenarios, dealt from one uniform permutation of each sample (one per stratum on law ST). The in-sample scenarios use no folds | `cvFolds = 10`. A stub replaces drtmle's one call to `sample`, so its folds are the dealt ones |
| reduced regression | the gate study's polynomial of $\operatorname{logit} g$, of a degree for each law | `glm_Qr` with the same formula. The runner checks drtmle's initial $Q_r$ against a `glm` refit on the supplied folds to $10^{-10}$ |
| targeting | the targeted fit (relative score tolerance $10^{-8}$, at most 100 rounds) and, on the cross-fitted scenarios, the one-step fit | `tolIC = 1e-8`, `maxIter = 100`, `tolg` at the key's lower bound |
| standard errors | the influence-curve standard error. With an estimated mechanism the package reports it as a plug-in diagnostic, not as inference | the variance of the curve rebuilt from drtmle's nuisances |

The two sides run the same arithmetic in sample and in the one-step form. On a cross-fitted
targeted fit they differ after the first round. drtmle moves each row along its own fold's
$Q_r / g$, and the package moves each fold's design along that fold's covariate, so the
targeted estimates differ at order $1/n$. With a learner that reads its training data the two
are different estimators, so those keys are not paired. The
[contract page](../adaptive-iptw.md) names this difference D9.

Each implementation is read against the exact truth on its own. Only the five known-mechanism
scenarios (`pd_known_*` and `st_known`) make an interval claim. Every other scenario has an
estimated mechanism and is point-only: its rows pass on bias alone, and their coverage and SE
ratio come from the plug-in standard error and carry no verdict.

The similarity rule above averages over replicates, so the per-replicate rule in
[`agreement.csv`](https://github.com/esbraun/cleverly-tmle/blob/main/tests/canonical/adaptive_iptw_pairing/agreement.csv)
carries the pairing verdict. A key agrees when the 99th percentile of the gap in SEs and of the
relative SE difference both stay under its bound, and the two initial estimates agree to
$10^{-10}$. The bound is $10^{-4}$ SE in sample and for every one-step form, where both sides
run the same arithmetic. It is 0.05 SE for a cross-fitted targeted fit on laws PD, ST and binary
C, where the tilt difference D9 is of order $1/n$. The measured-values table below gives the
counts and the largest gap under each bound.

On the three-arm C, CN and MA composites the cross-fitted targeted keys carry no verdict.
drtmle's per-row tilts merge the mechanism levels that the saturated $Q_r$ reads, and CN's first
step is large, so the targeted gap reached 0.07 to 0.22 SE in the 30-replicate pilot. These
keys were made descriptive after that pilot and before the declared run. Their one-step forms
run the same arithmetic on both sides and carry the verdict at $10^{-4}$.

A key whose mean targeting movement is under ten times its bound is labelled "targeting not
witnessed", because agreement there could not see a missing step. One key carries the label:
`ey_regime[s]` of `pd_known_regimes`, whose stochastic regime moves the estimate by 0.43 SE on
average against a threshold of 0.5.

drtmle lost one replicate: on replicate 271 of `pd_regimes_insample` a mechanism reached its trim
$[\text{tolg}, 1 - \text{tolg}]$, which the runner records as a failure. The study allows 10 per
cell. No `cleverly` fit failed.

## Limitations

| limit | what it means for use |
| --- | --- |
| no pairing for a cross-fitted mechanism learner that reads its training data | drtmle trains fold $k$'s $Q_r$ on a supplied out-of-fold mechanism that saw fold $k$, and the package trains it on leave-two-folds-out designs. They are different estimators, and the leave-two-out refit has no external comparison |
| three-arm C, CN and MA targeted keys are descriptive | their cross-fitted targeted gap is reported as `max_agreement_gap_descriptive`. The one-step forms agree, so the gap is the tilt difference D9, but no bound covers the targeted keys |
| the mechanism is this package's | drtmle receives the package's initial mechanism, so the pairing checks the reduced regression, the targeting and the curves. It does not check the mechanism learners, which the gate study reads against the truth |
| intervals only with a known mechanism | the estimated-mechanism scenarios are point-only. Their plug-in standard errors are paired as numbers, not as inference |
| one learner per law and no weights or clusters | the weighted and clustered laws of the gate study are not paired, because `adaptive_iptw` takes no weights or cluster identifiers |
