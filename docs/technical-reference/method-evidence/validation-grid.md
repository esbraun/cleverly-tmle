# Implementation validation grid

Every row is one registered validation study of one `cleverly` method. Read a row left to right
and it answers four questions in order. What does `cleverly` implement? What was it validated
against? Does it recover a known truth and behave as its theory predicts? What does the study
*not* establish?

The three count columns are three different questions and are not interchangeable. *Accuracy vs
known truth* tests each implementation against the law on its own, which is what stops two
implementations agreeing with each other from counting as evidence; where a canonical
implementation exists, half of those tests measure it rather than `cleverly`. *`cleverly` vs
canonical* is the paired comparison. *Theory properties* is the declared repeated-sampling
behaviour, which a matching implementation would not establish even if the match were exact.

`tests/unit/test_method_evidence.py` requires one row per registered validation study and one
study per row, requires every link in a row to reach that study's own registered section, and
derives every count and headline number below from the committed result files instead
of reading them.

Use either horizontal scrollbar to move through columns. Hover over a shortened cell to read its
full content. Keyboard users can focus the cell and press Enter. Use the **Studies** list to move
to a registered study.

A registered study with no canonical comparator says so in its own cell and rests on the other two
columns. A comparator-backed study starts its `cleverly` vs canonical cell with
"comparator-backed". Each such study declares its comparison kind, and its agreement block names
the kind of every row.

The grid lists a study that declares property cells or a reference. A
comparator-backed study with no property cells reads "0/0 cells; the row rests on the comparison
and the truth tests" in its theory-properties cell. The
[learned-rule boundary study](learned-rule-cvtmle-boundary.md) has neither. It has its own page
and no row here.

A study with a pending comparator carries the label "Python property evidence, with comparator
runs pending" and a paired count of 0/0.

Full test-by-test results are in the
[implementation validation studies](index.md). Each study publishes one row per
committed test, with what the test checked and the verdict its own endpoints produced.
