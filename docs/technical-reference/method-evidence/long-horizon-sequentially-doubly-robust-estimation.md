# Long-horizon sequentially doubly robust estimation

This study validates `SDR` with binary and three-level treatment nodes at three, four and five decisions, in
sample and cross-fitted over five folds. The regression at the horizon is the declared quasibinomial GLM. Every
earlier regression is least squares on the transform, unclipped. The laws come from
`tests/discrete_law_longitudinal_horizon.py`, which gives the exact truth of every estimand. The declared
comparator is R [`lmtp`](https://www.jstatsoft.org/article/view/v102i09) with `cf_sdr`.

Its runs are pending as roadmap item
[X59](../../roadmap.md#x59-long-horizon-comparator-parity-for-ltmle-and-sdr). This page holds Python property
evidence, with comparator runs pending.

The study publishes under the `reporting` policy. The design lists 46 gated truth rows and 32 gated
calibration cells before any run (`tests/long_horizon_design.py`). A red gated row or cell fails
`tests/unit/test_long_horizon_design.py`. A row outside the list reports its measured value and blocks nothing. The
support rows `G46.sdr.*` in `src/cleverly/_support_rows.py` cite this study.

One reporting row is red. The section [Red cell of this study](#red-cell-of-this-study) states it.

## What was compared

Each scenario draws 1,200 replications at the $n$ in the table. A law is written $(B, s, c_0)$.

| scenario | law | $n$ | plans |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `sdr_binary_k3` | $L_R$ | 4,000 | `never`, `always`, `respond`, `switch`, `coin` |
| `sdr_binary_k4` | $L_R$ | 16,000 | the same five plans |
| `sdr_binary_k5` | $L_R$ | 76,000 | the same five plans |
| `sdr_categorical_k5` | $L_R$ | 116,000 | `low`, `respond`, `mix`, `mixlast` |
| `sdr_categorical_k5_crossfit` | $L_R$ | 116,000 | the plans of `sdr_categorical_k5`, at five folds on its realized sample |
| `sdr_categorical_k5_static` | (0.5, 0.05, 3.5) | 124,000 | `low`, `high`, `switch` |
| `sdr_categorical_k5_switch` | (0.5, 0.1, 3.5) | 72,000 | `low`, `switch` |
| `sdr_categorical_k5_high` | (0.25, 0, 3.5) | 64,000 | `low`, `high` |
| `sdr_categorical_k5_mix` | (1, 0.05, 3.5) | 96,000 | `low`, `mix` |
| `sdr_policy_k5` | $L_R$ | 44,000 | `low`, `mix`, `taper` |
| `sdr_policy_k5_mix` | (1, 0.05, 3.5) | 96,000 | `low`, `mix`, `taper` |
| `sdr_policy_k5_taper` | (1, 0, 3.5) | 76,000 | `low`, `taper` |

The policy scenarios also report the paired contrast of `taper` against `mix`, and the categorical scenarios
report `mix` against `mixlast`. `tests/longitudinal_horizon_plans.py` states each plan as the regimen that `SDR`
takes.

| setting | `cleverly` | R `lmtp` |
| --- | --- | --- |
| mechanism | the law's treatment and censoring probabilities, passed as probability columns | pending under X59 |
| bounds | `g_bounds=(1e-8, 1)` | pending under X59 |
| regressions | quasibinomial GLM at the horizon, least squares on the transform at every earlier node | pending under X59 |
| cross-fitting | five folds in `sdr_categorical_k5_crossfit` | pending under X59 |
| intervals | Wald for every row. A single-fold scenario also writes a BCa row from 199 bootstrap refits | pending under X59 |

## Limitations

| limit | what it means for use |
| --- | --- |
| one law family with binary and three-level nodes | the laws are the $(B, s, c_0)$ family of `tests/discrete_law_longitudinal_horizon.py`. The study does not cover more than three arms, continuous covariates or a continuous outcome |
| the comparator runs are pending | no external implementation checks these rows. Roadmap item X59 adds R `lmtp` |
| the declared mechanism is exact | the law's probabilities enter as columns, so the study measures the regressions with the mechanism known. The `learned_mechanism` rows report two learned cases and gate nothing |
| one reporting Wald row is red | `ey_regimen[switch]` in `sdr_categorical_k5_switch` covers 0.9133 at an $n$ below its tail size. The row reports, and its BCa row passes |
| the calibration count is below the 0.95 family pass probability | the owner chose 8,000 replications. The four low three-level arms read SE ratios of 0.954 to 0.972 in the pilot. A reader must treat the 99% interval of each calibration row as the evidence, not the count |
| the Wald interval gates only at $n \ge 2 n_\kappa$ | below that size the Wald row reports and the BCa row gates. A reader who needs a valid interval at a smaller $n$ must read the BCa row |
| the cross-fitted scenario reports Wald rows only | the cross-fitted bootstrap is experimental (X20). The cross-fitted rows gate through `interval_calibration` cells at $2 n_\kappa$ |
| the bootstrap band is not gated | the study has no `simultaneous_coverage` cell. The bootstrap band of `SDR` stays experimental |
| support loss is reported and not gated | the `support_loss` rows report the binding share per node. The study states no rule for how much support loss an estimate tolerates |

The causal interpretation requires consistency, sequential exchangeability, longitudinal positivity, and
conditionally independent censoring. The calibration, bias and rate families test the validity of the interval
and the estimate under those conditions. They do not test the conditions.
