# Repeated-outcome longitudinal SDR

This study validates `SDR` with `outcome=Repeated(["Y1", "Y2", "Y3"])`. A repeated outcome is
measured at every node and is not absorbing. The parameter is the mean $E[Y_h^d]$ for each plan $d$
and horizon $h$. Each horizon is the end-of-study fit of the panel truncated after node $h$. The
functional is Equation (4) of Díaz, Williams, Hoffman and Schenck (2023) on that prefix. The section
[repeated outcomes](../longitudinal-tmle.md#repeated-outcomes) states the estimator and its
exact-law evidence. The declared comparator is R
[`lmtp`](https://cran.r-project.org/package=lmtp) 1.5.4 with `cf_sdr`.

Its runs are pending as the TRACKER Study queue item of plan X32b, PR C. This page holds Python property evidence, with comparator runs pending.

The study reports each unique parameter once. The dynamic plan equals the always-treated plan at the
first node, so its mean and contrast at $t = 1$ duplicate those of `always`. A fast test checks that
identity. The duplicates are not cells of this study.

## What was compared

The R column states the declared design of the pending comparator runs.

| setting | `cleverly` | R `lmtp` |
| --- | --- | --- |
| datasets | 2,400 censored panels of 12,000 rows per scenario from `make_longitudinal_repeated`, with three nodes | the identical rows |
| fit | one `SDR` fit with `outcome=Repeated(["Y1", "Y2", "Y3"])` | one `cf_sdr` call for each plan and horizon $h$ on the panel truncated after node $h$. The earlier $Y_s$ are covariates |
| scenarios | `repeated_folds_1` (in sample) and `repeated_folds_5` (five folds) | the same fold assignment, read from the committed `fold` column |
| plans | `never`, `always`, and a rule that treats at node 1 and then treats exactly when the previous outcome was positive | the same three plans, as per-node shifted values |
| reported parameters | the mean of each plan at $t = 1, 2, 3$, and the contrasts of the other two plans against `never`. That is eight means and five contrasts | the same, 13 unique cells |
| mechanism | the generating treatment and censoring probabilities, read through a declared design width, with `g_bounds` from the study constant | the per-node ratios of the same probabilities, built by `pairing_sample` |
| sequential regressions | cell means keyed on the earlier outcomes $Y_{<t}$ among the followers of the plan | the same cell means, keyed on $(Y_{<t}, A_{\le t})$ |
| intervals | pointwise 95% Wald from the influence curve | the influence curve of `cf_sdr` |
| initial estimate | the mean of the first node's regression of the corrected chain | the plug-in that the adapter returns beside the estimate |

The regression key omits $W$ and $L$, so the regression is wrong on this law. The correction is
then nonzero in every replication at both fold counts. With the mechanisms known, `SDR` is
consistent for any regression (Díaz et al. 2023, Lemma 4), so the truth gates are valid.

Each truth is an exact enumeration of the binary law (`cleverly.datasets.repeated_truth`).
`tests/studies/ltmle_repeated_law.py` repeats the enumeration on the observed-data law.

The pairing plug-in misses the truth by more than 0.25 empirical standard deviations in 11 of the 13
cells at $n$ = 12,000. The two means of the rule at $t = 2$ and $t = 3$ miss by 0.15 and 0.24. No
sampled row shows that the correction is needed for those two cells. The exact witnesses `E2`, `E3`
and `N8'` in `tests/unit/test_influence_gateaux_repeated.py` and
`tests/unit/test_repeated_outcome_sdr_mutations.py` carry that claim.

The primary $n$ = 12,000 is the smallest multiple of 1,000 at which the expected number of
unseen-cell fits over the run is at most 0.01. R `lmtp` returns `NA` on an unseen cell.

There is no mixed-pattern family. `SDR` and `LTMLE` differ by at most 0.34 empirical standard
deviations at $n$ = 2,000 on this law in such patterns, so the family would measure nothing. The
exact witness `E2b` records sequential double robustness (Lemma 4) at a mixed pattern.

In sample, a saturated outcome learner reproduces every seen cell. The correction is then zero on
seen cells. The in-sample calibration cells test the plug-in and the mechanism terms. The
`double_robustness` and `correction_necessity` families use a regression on $W$ alone, so their
correction is nonzero. The tests `E2`, `E3` and `N8'` cover the correction exactly.

The in-sample cells use $n$ = 32,000. At $n$ = 8,000 the in-sample standard error ran below the
sampling spread for the rule, with SE ratios of 0.920 for `rule_t3` and 0.897 for the continuous
arm. The cause is the loss of residual variance in a cell of $k$ units by the factor
$(k - 1)/k$. The deficit falls as $1/n$. The ratio of `rule_t3` is 0.857, 0.907, 0.944, 0.966 and
0.984 at $n$ = 2,000, 4,000, 8,000, 16,000 and 32,000. `LTMLE` with the same learners gives the
same standard error to 1e-6, and a GLM outcome regression gives ratios of 0.98 to 1.00 at every
$n$. So the study doubled the in-sample and continuous sizes twice and re-ran on fresh seeds. The
`small_sample_calibration` cells report the shortfall at $n$ = 2,000.

Two families show SE ratios below 1 with a wrong mechanism: 0.763 for `rule_t3__outcome_correct`
and 0.772 for `rule_t3__full_history` in the pilots. The Wald standard error is inconsistent under
a wrong mechanism. These families gate bias only and make no calibration claim.

## Limits

| limit | what it means for use |
| --- | --- |
| one law with three nodes and a binary outcome | the continuous outcome has one calibration arm, and the rate in $n$ is not measured |
| the comparator runs are pending | the claim rests on exact truths, exact-law witnesses and the controls. The pending R `lmtp` runs add a paired check of the estimate and the influence curve on the same draws |
| the primary fit declares the mechanisms | the primary cells and the paired comparison do not claim parity for learned treatment or censoring models. The property cells use fitted quasibinomial mechanisms |
| the in-sample interval runs below nominal at small $n$ | the `small_sample_calibration` cells report the shortfall at $n$ = 2,000 and no rule gates them. The gated in-sample cells draw $n$ = 32,000 |
| the cross-fitted learners are one cell-mean regression and one shallow booster | the study does not cover a larger learner library or a different fold count than five |
| a wrong mechanism gives no interval claim | the `double_robustness` and `full_history_necessity` families gate bias only. The Wald standard error is inconsistent there |
| the absorbing, history-free and Markov controls read exact limits of this law | they show that the repeated reading, the earlier outcomes and the depth of the outcome regression matter here. They do not bound the difference on another law |
| the second-order dependence acts at node 3 only | the law has three nodes, so the depth-two terms act at the last node. No cell reads a longer history |
| no weights, clusters, working model or survival outcome in this study | the [sequentially doubly robust study](sequentially-doubly-robust-estimation.md) measures the end-of-study and survival outcomes |

The causal interpretation requires consistency, sequential exchangeability, longitudinal
positivity, and conditionally independent censoring.
