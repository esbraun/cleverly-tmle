# Learned-rule contrasts with stochastic regimes and the natural course

This study validates the intervals of a learned-rule fit that compares the rule with a known
stochastic regime or with the natural course. The estimator is
`TMLE(learned_rule=LearnedRule(), interventions=[...], n_folds=10, targeting_scheme="pooled",
g_bounds="auto")`, the engine form of `LearnedRuleContrast(against=(...))`. Each fold learns the
plug-in rule $d_v$ of its outcome regression on its training rows. The rule is column 0 of the
fit's regime set and each known regime one more column, on the same folds and the same fitted
$g_v$, and one pooled fluctuation targets every column. A known stochastic regime enters as its
density $g^*(a \mid W)$. The natural course is not a column: its value is the fold mean of the
observed outcome, $\sum_v \omega_v \bar Y_v$, with curve $Y - \bar Y_{v(i)}$, outside the
fluctuation.

A contrast's curve is $D_L - D_C$, the difference of the learned and the comparator curve on the
same rows, and the report's variance rule is applied to it.
The stochastic-regime subsection of [Learned rules](../point-treatment-tmle.md#learned-rules)
gives the construction and the conditions.

The learned-rule target is data-adaptive: $\tilde\psi_{0n} = \sum_v \omega_v \Psi_{d_v}(P_0)$,
with $\omega_v = 1/V$ under the fold-evaluated report and $n_v/n$ under the stacked one. A known
regime's target is $E_0 \sum_a g^*(a \mid W) \bar Q_0(a, W)$, the natural course's is $E_0 Y$, and
a contrast's is the difference. Each replication carries its own truth, and every statistic reads
the error, the estimate minus that truth.
[A truth that varies by replication](../../development/method-benchmarking.md#a-truth-that-varies-by-replication)
gives the rule.

**No canonical implementation is compared.** No maintained package fits a cross-validated TMLE
of a learned rule beside these comparators. A zero-row equivalence artifact records the absence
of a comparator.

## What was tested

| setting | declaration |
| --- | --- |
| laws | the RM30 laws of the [gated study](learned-rule-cvtmle.md#what-was-tested): $W_1 \sim U(-1, 1)$, $W_2 \sim \operatorname{Bernoulli}(0.5)$, $\operatorname{logit} g_0(1 \mid W) = 0.3 W_1 - 0.2 W_2$, and $\operatorname{logit} \bar Q_0(a, W) = 0.2 + 0.5 W_1 - 0.3 W_2 + a\, b(W)$ |
| fits | two per draw at n = 2,000, on one fold seed, under both reports (`cv_evaluation=True`, fold-evaluated, the primary report, and `cv_evaluation=False`, stacked). Fit C: beside `[Static(0), coin, offer, NaturalCourse()]`, where `coin` treats with probability 0.5 and `offer` treats with probability 0.5 where $W_1 > 0$, both `Stochastic(..., density_kind="known")`. Fit N: beside `[NaturalCourse()]` alone. `repeats=1` and pointwise intervals |
| outcome learner | `Pipeline(PolynomialFeatures(2, interaction_only=True, include_bias=False), LogisticRegression(C=1e6, max_iter=5000, tol=1e-10))` on $(A, W_1, W_2)$ |
| treatment learner | `LogisticRegression(C=1e6, max_iter=1000)` on $(W_1, W_2)$, a correct parametric model fitted by maximum likelihood |
| support keys | every fit is checked: family `P11`, `splitting` `("fold_evaluated",)` or `("stacked",)`, and the qualifiers `known_regimes` with `known_stochastic_regimes` and `natural_course` (fit C) or `natural_course` (fit N) |
| truth | per replication for the learned value: refit each fold's outcome learner on the fit's training rows with the package's `fit_on_rows`, require the fit's rule on every validation row, integrate each fold rule's value by the trapezoid rule on 4,001 points of $W_1$ for each $W_2$, and weight fold $v$ by the report's $\omega_v$. Each comparator's truth is fixed, by the same rule |
| harness checks | both fits draw the same folds and rules, and fit N's natural course is fit C's number |
| primary budget | 1,600 replications of `non_exceptional`, fold-evaluated report, all eleven parameters |
| primary verdicts | the framework defaults on the error: the 99% Student bias interval inside 0.25 SD, the lower end of the 99% Clopper-Pearson coverage interval at or above 0.90, and the SE-ratio interval inside 0.80 to 1.20 |
| seeds | `seed=20265809` and `resampling_seed=20265810` |
| policy | `gated` |

| law | $b(W)$ | what the outcome learner can represent | role | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| `non_exceptional` | $0.1 + W_1$ | the blip: the model is correct | primary and `interval_calibration` | 2,000 |
| `misspecified_limit` | $0.8 W_1 + W_1^2 - 0.3$ | no $W_1^2$ term, so the treated arm is misspecified | `conservative_coverage` | 2,000 |

On `non_exceptional` the comparators' values are 0.51218 for `Static(0)`, 0.52190 for `coin`,
0.54434 for `offer` and 0.52657 for $E Y$; on `misspecified_limit`, 0.51151, 0.53340 and 0.51558
for `coin`, `offer` and $E Y$. The laws, the learners, the budgets, the seeds, the truth rule, the
sizing rule and the support-row condition were fixed before any run. The calibration count was then set by the per-arm rule on a 4,000-draw pilot, declared
before the pilot: 6,400 at ideal ratios, raised to 10,000 by the pilot's ratios before any
declared draw. `tests/unit/test_learned_rule_contrast_comparators_design.py` pins the truths, the
efficiency bounds, the predicted SE ratios, the sizing, the seeds and the declared support rows.
`tests/unit/test_learned_rule_contrast_study.py` and `tests/unit/test_learned_rule_natural_course.py`
hold the unit witnesses of the construction.

| `independent_se_diagnostic` | how each contrast covers with the SE $\sqrt{se_L^2 + se_C^2}$ it would have if the two values were independent | nothing fails. The cells have the role `diagnostic`, and no margin is read |
| `targeting_necessity` | does the fluctuation remove the plug-in's bias of the contrast against `offer`, with 1,200 replications of `non_exceptional` under the fold-evaluated report | the positive arm fits RM30's `drop_w1` learner, saturated in $(A, W_2)$ with no $W_1$ term. cross-checks it by Monte Carlo. The coverage floor 0.92 is the calibration band's lower end. The
correlation-one ceiling of 0.80 lies below the claim's own floor of 0.92.
