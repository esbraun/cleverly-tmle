# Stratified incremental MSM

This study validates three stratified targets of ordinary point-treatment TMLE with baseline strata,
`strata=["V"]`: incremental propensity-score interventions, marginal structural models (MSMs) with
a logit link or a continuous dose, and the cross-fitted natural-course mean with a missing
outcome. A stratum parameter is the marginal parameter of the law inside one of a fixed number of
baseline strata. The construction is the marginal result applied inside each cell of a finite
partition, which the [natural-extension verdicts](../natural-extension-verdicts.md) record as part
(i).

Each target solves one score block $I(V=s) H_s / P_n(V=s)$ for each equation of its targeting.
Each stratum curve is $I(V=s) D_s / P_n(V=s)$. The incremental fit alternates outcome blocks and
treatment-mechanism blocks. The linked MSM fit takes its stratum coefficients from a nested
fluctuation of the blocks at each stratum's own coefficients.
[Point-treatment TMLE](../point-treatment-tmle.md#weights-strata-and-clusters) states each
construction.

## What was tested

| setting | declaration |
| --- | --- |
| law L1 | the [baseline-strata law](stratified-point-treatment-tmle.md): $V \in \{0, 1, 2\}$ with probabilities 0.5, 0.3 and 0.2, $W \in \{0, 1, 2\}$ given $V$, a binary treatment and a binary outcome. `tests/studies/stratified_alternating_law.py` computes every truth and every efficient influence function exactly over the 36 support points |
| law L2 | L1's $V$ and $W$, a dose $A \sim N(0.5 W - 0.4 V, 1)$, a continuous outcome for the identity link and a binary outcome for the logit link, both with an $A V$ term |
| L1 with a missing outcome | L1, with the outcome observed with probability $\operatorname{expit}(1.1 - 0.3 A + 0.2 W - 0.25 V)$ |
| primary scenario | `stratified_incremental`: the incremental means at odds multipliers 1, 2 and 0.5 and their two contrasts, marginal and in each stratum, $n = 2000$, 1,600 replications |
| fits | in sample, with main-terms logistic regressions on $(A, W, V)$ and $(W, V)$, which are correct for L1. The natural-course fits are cross-fitted on ten folds |
| Monte Carlo inference | exact 99% intervals around every rate, and 99% intervals around every primary endpoint |

The comparator is R `npcausal` `ipsi` at commit `56a5ac1`. `npcausal` has no stratified
estimator, so the runner calls `ipsi` once per stratum subset and multiplier, and once per
multiplier on every row for the marginal means. The two implementations differ in two declared
ways.

| difference | effect |
| --- | --- |
| `npcausal` cross-fits its nuisances over two splits. Its single-split path selects no training rows at `56a5ac1` | the paired difference scales like $1/n_s$, so it is largest in the smallest stratum |
| `npcausal` fits `SL.glm.interaction`. Inside a stratum it is saturated. On every row it has no $A{:}W{:}V$ term, so it is correct for L1 but not saturated | the marginal rows compare two correct but different regressions |

The MSM and natural-course targets have no stratified comparator. The property cells measure them
against the exact truths.

The property grid has six families.

| family | what it measures |
| --- | --- |
| `interval_calibration` | the fifteen stratum incremental parameters; the logit MSM $(1, a, W)$, marginal and in each stratum; and the cross-fitted natural-course mean, marginal and in each stratum. Each cell has a shrunken-SE control and a noise control |
| `simultaneous_coverage` | the band over the twenty incremental parameters of one fit |
| `stratum_targeting_necessity` | the stratified incremental fit against the unstratified fit's mixture inside a stratum, with an outcome regression that omits $V$ |
| `targeting_necessity` | the stratified logit-MSM slope against the untargeted stratum projection of the same initial regression |
| `double_robustness` | the logit MSM $(1, a)$ in each stratum under four nuisance configurations, and the dose MSM coefficients with a correct outcome regression |

The robustness family fits $(1, a)$, not $(1, a, W)$. Inside a stratum the logit model
$(1, a, W)$ contains the outcome regression, so a fit with both nuisances wrong stays within
0.5 SD of the truth, and its control could not fail. The dose cells are robustness cells and not
calibration cells. The package fits the dose density with bins, which is not the law's density,
so no result predicts a calibrated standard error.

Two positive cells are red, and both measure the logit-MSM slope:
`interval_calibration/logit_v1_a__correctly_specified` and `logit_v2_a__correctly_specified`.
Their SE-ratio intervals end at 0.927 and 0.939 against a floor of 0.93, and both cover
nominally. Stratum 2 holds about 400 of the 2,000 rows.

The owner `X8-logit-small-stratum` in the [roadmap](../../roadmap.md#red-cell-owners) records the
diagnosis. A declared diagnostic fitted the shipped unstratified logit MSM on samples of
stratum 2's size from the law given $V = 2$. It under-reports alike, 0.896 against the stratified
fit's 0.867 on fresh draws. At four times the size both read 0.997 and 1.006. The study declared
`gated` and moved to `reporting` before a re-run at the same seeds, which its declaration allows
for a finite-sample red with no defect.

## Limits

- The study covers two finite-support laws with one three-level stratum, and one continuous-dose
  law with the same strata.
- The incremental and MSM fits are in sample. The cross-fitted forms of these targets are not
  measured here.
- The dose fits use the package's binned density on six bins. They publish no calibration cell
  and no band.
- The MSM and natural-course targets have no stratified comparator, so only the truth measures
  them.
- `targeting_scheme="fold"` and `cv_evaluation=True` refuse strata, so neither is measured.
