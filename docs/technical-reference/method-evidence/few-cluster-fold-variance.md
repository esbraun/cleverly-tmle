# The centred fold cluster variance at few clusters per fold

This study validates the interval of a fold-evaluated `TMLE` (`cv_evaluation=True`) fitted with
`id=`. Its variance centres the cluster totals of the curve inside each validation fold and pools
them, and its interval takes $t_{J-V}$ ([few clusters](../inference.md#few-clusters)). The study
reads that interval by training clusters, the clusters outside a validation fold that fit its
models, and measures the floor from which it is calibrated.

**Status of this page.** The study is registered, and its run has completed. No other
implementation computes this variance, so the study has no comparator. The publication policy is
`reporting`. Three positive cells at 2 training clusters are red, below each configuration's
training floor of 4, as the declared route reads them. They stay red in the
[red-cell ledger](red-cells.md) under the owner `X54-training-floor`, and
`MINIMUM_FOLD_TRAINING_CLUSTERS` ships the floor.

| part | what it is | status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| primary | `equal10` with $V = 5$ and $J = 20$ (16 training clusters), 1,000 replications: the two arm means and the ATE | the accuracy table below. Every row passes |
| property family `fold_variance_calibration` | twelve cells, two laws by six designs $(V, J)$, on 8,000 draws each. Every draw fits twice, once with the propensity estimated and once with it declared known, and reads eight arms off the two fits | the tables below give the verdicts |
| comparator runs | none | no other implementation centres the cluster totals within folds |

## What was fitted

Law `equal10` holds equal clusters of 10 rows with a cluster latent in the outcome and an
informative propensity, and its cells measure the ATE. Law `cluster_covariate` adds a cluster-level
covariate to the outcome, and its cells measure the arm-1 mean, which carries the cluster effect
that the ATE nearly cancels. The designs are $(V, J)$ = (2, 4), (2, 8), (2, 12), (5, 10), (5, 20)
and (5, 30). They give 2, 4 and 6 clusters per validation fold at each $V$, and 2, 4, 6, 8, 16 and
24 training clusters, the axis each configuration's floor is read on.

Each fit is `TMLE(cv_evaluation=True, targeting_scheme="pooled")` with grouped folds, unpenalized
logistic regressions in $W_1$ and $W_2$, propensity bounds (0.01, 0.99), and `id=`. The
`known_propensity` configuration refits the same draw with the law's propensity declared through
`treatment_probabilities=`. The laws, sizes and seeds are in
`tests/studies/few_cluster_fold_variance.py`, and `tests/unit/test_few_cluster_fold_variance_design.py`
recomputes them.

## Limitations

- **Equal clusters and equal folds.** Every design has equal cluster sizes and $J/V$ clusters in
  every validation fold. Unequal folds make $t_{J-V}$ an approximation.
- **Parametric learners.** Every fit uses unpenalized logistic regressions on main terms. Wang et
  al. (2024), Remark 3, caution against flexible learners at about 20 clusters.
- **Two laws, binary outcomes, a binary treatment.** The ATE on `equal10` and the arm-1 mean on
  `cluster_covariate`; no weights, strata or missing outcomes.
- **Two fold counts.** $V = 2$ and 5 only, with 2 to 6 clusters per validation fold.
- **No comparator.** No other implementation centres the cluster totals within folds.
