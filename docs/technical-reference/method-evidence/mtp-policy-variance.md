# MTP policy variance

This study validates `variance="policy_weighted"` on `LTMLE` and `SDR` for a modified treatment
policy, and it checks the default Wald interval in the regime where its tail-size rule says the
interval can be read. The option keeps the default estimate. Its standard error comes from
$\max(\hat\sigma^2_{IC}, \hat\sigma^2_{pw})$, where $\hat\sigma^2_{pw}$ is a sequential plug-in of
the variance of the efficient influence curve that reads the density ratio at each row's policy
dose (`cleverly.longitudinal.policy_variance`). The interval therefore contains the default Wald
interval in every sample. The
[interval under heavy cumulative density ratios](../longitudinal-tmle.md#the-interval-under-heavy-cumulative-density-ratios)
section states the estimator, its guarantee and its conditions.

**Status of this page.** The study is registered, and its Python phases have run. R `lmtp` has no
such variance, so the study has no comparator and no pending reference. The publication policy is
`gated`: every gated row passes. A red cell of the default interval stays red in the
[red-cell ledger](red-cells.md), with the owner row that names it.

| Python property evidence | the option on the heavy-ratio cells of [`mtp-pricing`](mtp-pricing.md) and [`mtp-boundaries`](mtp-boundaries.md), with four controls per positive, and calibration twins of the default interval | the tables below give the verdicts. It decides where the option is supported |

## What was compared

The tail-size rule of
[`X39-below-tail-size`](red-cells.md) reads a Wald interval only at n of at least $2 n_\kappa$,
with $n_\kappa = \lceil (\kappa - 1) / 0.25^2 \rceil$ and $\kappa$ the kurtosis of the efficient
influence curve under the law. Several passing rows of the two source studies sit below that size,
and the five-node `price_down` rows of `mtp-pricing` sit far below it. The primary phase draws
1,800 samples of each twin at its own size. The `tau = 3` and atom twins also fit the option and
publish its rows from the same fits.

| scenario | n | source rows twinned |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `pricing_ltmle_tau5_tail` | 72,800 | `mtp-pricing` `pricing_ltmle_tau5`, `price_down` |
| `pricing_sdr_tau5_tail` | 73,600 | `pricing_sdr_tau5`, `price_down` |
| `pricing_ltmle_tau3_tail` | 5,600 | `pricing_ltmle_tau3`, `price_down` |
| `pricing_sdr_tau3_tail` | 5,600 | `pricing_sdr_tau3`, `price_down` |
| `atom_ltmle_tail` | 6,400 | `mtp-boundaries` `atom_ltmle`, `ate_regimen[p5 vs natural]` |
| `atom_sdr_tail` | 6,400 | `atom_sdr`, `ate_regimen[p5 vs natural]` |
| `p4_ltmle_tail` | 104,400 | `boundaries_ltmle`, plan `p4`, the exact law-B ratio |
| `p4_sdr_tail` | 104,400 | `boundaries_sdr`, plan `p4`, the exact law-B ratio |

The laws, plans and truths are those of the source studies (`tests/studies/mtp_pricing_common.py`
for law P, `tests/studies/mtp_boundaries_common.py` for law B and law B'). The twin sizes are
derived in the design test from the committed tail-size tables of
`tests/diagnostics/fix5_heavy_ratios/`, never typed in. A twin passes on its bias interval and the
0.90 coverage floor.

The regressions inside $\hat\sigma^2_{pw}$ are boosted trees of depth 5. A screen chose that depth
before any gated cell ran, on 400 draws of each of 16 held-out configurations: the largest SE-ratio
point was 1.106 at depth 5 against 1.162 at depth 3, and both stayed inside the band
(`tests/studies/mtp_policy_variance_screen.csv.gz`). The gated laws were held out of the screen.

regime: at 1,800 draws the floor passes a true coverage of 0.92 with probability 0.62. The study
reads each `pricing_*_tau5_tail` row against the n = 5,000 Wald control of the same estimator and
quantity, with a 99% Newcombe interval on the difference (`regime_contrast` in
`tests/studies/canonical_mtp_policy_variance.py`, recomputed from the committed rows by
`tests/unit/test_mtp_policy_variance_registration.py`).

measured gain over n = 5,000. The `LTMLE` contrast twin's coverage interval, 0.912 to 0.944, lies
below 0.95. The declaration narrates a shortfall of a truth twin only after a 3,000-draw
confirmation, which has not run, so this page states the number and draws no conclusion from it.
