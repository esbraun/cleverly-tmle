# Long-horizon longitudinal TMLE

This study validates `LTMLE` with binary treatment nodes at three, four and five decisions. It also
validates the duration working model of the marginal structural model (MSM) at five decisions. The laws come
from `tests/discrete_law_longitudinal_horizon.py`, which gives the exact truth of every estimand. The
declared comparator is R [`ltmle`](https://www.jstatsoft.org/article/view/v081i01) 1.3-0.

Its runs are pending as roadmap item
[X59](../../roadmap.md#x59-long-horizon-comparator-parity-for-ltmle-and-sdr). This page holds Python property
evidence, with comparator runs pending.

The study publishes under the `reporting` policy. The design lists 28 gated truth rows and 16 gated
calibration cells before any run (`tests/long_horizon_design.py`). A red gated row or cell fails
`tests/unit/test_long_horizon_design.py`. A row outside the list reports its measured value and blocks nothing. The
support rows `G46.tmle.*` in `src/cleverly/_support_rows.py` cite this study.

## What was compared

Each scenario draws 3,000 replications at the $n$ in the table. The $n$ is the largest exact-law tail size
$n_\kappa$ of the rows that the scenario gates, rounded up to 4,000. The MSM scenario runs at twice its largest
$n_\kappa$, rounded up to 96,000, so that both coefficients are in the Wald regime.

| scenario | $n$ | plans | what it reports |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `binary_k3` | 4,000 | `never`, `always`, `respond`, `switch` | regimen means and contrasts at three decisions |
| `binary_k4` | 16,000 | the same four plans | the same at four decisions |
| `binary_k5` | 76,000 | the same four plans, `coin` and `coinlast` | the same at five decisions, and the paired contrast of `coin` against `coinlast` |
| `binary_k5_msm` | 96,000 | `dur0` to `dur5` | the intercept and duration coefficients of the working model intercept plus duration, with an identity link and unit weights |

| setting | `cleverly` | R `ltmle` 1.3-0 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| mechanism | the law's treatment and censoring probabilities, passed as probability columns | pending under X59 |
| bounds | `g_bounds=(1e-8, 1)` | pending under X59 |
| outcome fit | the declared quasibinomial GLM for each outcome and pseudo-outcome regression | pending under X59 |
| intervals | Wald for every row. A single-fold, non-MSM scenario also writes a BCa row from 199 bootstrap refits | pending under X59 |

`never` and `always` are static plans, `respond` and `switch` are dynamic, and `coin` and `coinlast` are
known stochastic plans. `tests/longitudinal_horizon_plans.py` states each plan as the regimen that `LTMLE` takes.

Every primary row reports against the exact truth of the law. The gated rows are the Wald rows that
`wald_gated` selects and the BCa rows that `interval_of` selects, 28 in all. The other rows report.

A single-fold fit without an MSM runs `n_bootstrap=199` with `bootstrap_interval="bca"`. It writes a Wald row
and a BCa row under the estimand name plus `__bca`. A Wald row gates only where the scenario $n$ is
at least twice the exact-law tail size $n_\kappa$ of the row. A BCa row gates only where the Wald row
does not gate. Where the Wald row gates, the BCa row reports. `tests/long_horizon_design.py` states the
rule in `interval_of`, and `tests/unit/test_long_horizon_design.py` checks the gated lists against it.
A cross-fitted fit reports Wald rows only, because the cross-fitted bootstrap is experimental (X20).

Each control must leave the band that its positive stays inside | $n$ = 4,000 to 116,000, set to $\mathrm{round\_n}(2 n_\kappa)$ of each cell, 6,000 replications |
| `root_n_and_efficiency` | `static_contrast` at $K$ = 4 on rungs of $n$ = 4,000, 12,000 and 48,000. The 4,000 rung is the control rung and carries no claim. The two `root_n_rate` rows fit the slope of the empirical SD and of the reported SE on $n$ | 1,000 replications per rung, 3,000 for the slope rows |
| `double_robustness` | `respond_vs_ref` at $K$ = 5: bias inside the margin when the outcome regression is correct, when the mechanism is correct, and when both are. The `both_wrong` control fits a wrong outcome regression with a constant mechanism and must miss the truth | $n$ = 40,000, 2,000 replications |
| `targeting_necessity` | the targeted fit against a plug-in with no fluctuation, for the plans `policy` and `respond` at $K$ = 3, 4, 5. The untargeted control is the plug-in fit with no fluctuation, and it must miss the truth. Each row answers to its own bias interval | $n$ = 4,000 to 16,000, 2,000 replications |
| `censoring_necessity` | `policy` at $K$ = 5 with the declared censoring mechanism against a control that sets the retention probability to one. The control must miss the truth | $n$ = 8,000 and 36,000, 2,000 replications |
| `rule_necessity` | the declared dynamic rule `respond` against its reversal. The reversed rule must miss the declared truth | $n$ = 4,000 and 116,000, 2,000 replications |
| `policy_necessity` | the declared stochastic policy against the control `mix_reversed`, which fits a reversed policy. The control must miss the declared truth | $n$ = 4,000 and 16,000, 2,000 replications |
| `type_i_error`, `power` | the paired contrast `coin` against `coinlast` at $K$ = 5: the sharp null must not reject above 0.10, and the alternative must reject at least 0.80 | $n$ = 52,000, 2,000 replications |
| `simultaneous_coverage` | the Wald-curve (Rademacher multiplier) band over the six $K$ = 5 plans, with its pointwise control. The study fits no bootstrap band | $n$ = 76,000, 4,000 replications |
| `support_loss` (reported) | the estimate with the default mechanism bounds against an unbounded fit, for `static` and `policy` at $K$ = 5. The table reports the binding share of each node. It states no verdict | $n$ = 4,000, 2,000 replications |
| `learned_mechanism` (reported) | `respond_vs_ref` at $K$ = 5 with a learned treatment mechanism. The table reports the summary statistics and states no verdict | $n$ = 40,000, 2,000 replications |

The bias families answer row by row: a positive passes when its bias interval lies inside the margin, and a
control passes when its interval lies wholly outside. A positive and its control run on independent streams at
their own $n$, so no row compares displacement between the two arms. The exact-law witnesses of
`tests/unit/test_long_horizon_design.py` set the control sizes.

**Replicate budgets.** The pilot merge sets each count (`tests/studies/long_horizon_ltmle_properties.py`).
Calibration takes 6,000 replications and the band takes 4,000. The bias families keep 2,000 although the bias
ladder reads 1,000.

**Red-cell reading.** The table states the declared reading of each family before any run, as follows.

| family | red when |
| --- | --- |
| `interval_calibration` | the 99% interval of the SE ratio leaves (0.93, 1.07) or the 99% coverage interval leaves (0.92, 0.98). A control passes by leaving the band |
| bias families | the 99% standardized-bias interval of a positive does not lie inside the margin, or that of a control does not lie wholly outside it |
| `root_n_and_efficiency` | a rung at 12,000 or 48,000 has a coverage lower limit below 0.90, an SE-ratio interval outside (0.80, 1.20), or a bias that the margin does not contain |
| `root_n_rate` | the slope interval is not within 0.125 of -0.5 or reaches -0.25 |
| `support_loss`, `learned_mechanism` | never. These rows report their summary statistics and state no verdict |

A calibration positive that fails reads as a defect suspect. It becomes a finite-sample reading with an owner
row only when its SE-ratio interval contains 1, its coverage interval contains 0.95, and the diagnosis finds no
defect. The run never adds replications to a red positive.

## Limitations

| limit | what it means for use |
| --- | --- |
| one law family with binary nodes | the laws are the `L_R` family of `tests/discrete_law_longitudinal_horizon.py`. The study does not cover continuous covariates or a continuous outcome |
| the comparator runs are pending | no external implementation checks these rows. Roadmap item X59 adds R `ltmle` 1.3-0 for the ordinary and MSM scenarios |
| the declared mechanism is exact | the law's probabilities enter as columns, so the study measures the targeting and outcome regressions with the mechanism known. The `learned_mechanism` row reports one learned case and gates nothing |
| the outcome regressions are one quasibinomial GLM | the exact-law criterion reads the declared GLM curve. The study does not cover a flexible learner library |
| the Wald interval gates only at $n \ge 2 n_\kappa$ | below that size the Wald row reports and the BCa row gates. A reader who needs a valid interval at a smaller $n$ must read the BCa row |
| the MSM scenario reports Wald rows only | the MSM fit runs no bootstrap, so a coefficient below $2 n_\kappa$ would have no gated interval. Both coefficients run above that size |
| no cross-fitted fit | this study fits in sample. The cross-fitted binary fit has no scenario here, and the cross-fitted bootstrap is experimental (X20) |
| support loss is reported and not gated | the `support_loss` rows report the binding share per node. The study states no rule for how much support loss an estimate tolerates |
| the simultaneous band is the Wald-curve band | the study gates the Rademacher multiplier band of the influence curves. The studentized bootstrap band is in the categorical study |

The causal interpretation requires consistency, sequential exchangeability, longitudinal positivity, and
conditionally independent censoring. The calibration, bias and rate families test the validity of the interval
and the estimate under those conditions. They do not test the conditions.
