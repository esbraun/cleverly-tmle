The [published Benkeser et al. article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5793673/)
defines the adjusted mean for a binary indicator and gives conditional asymptotic inference.
Applying that functional to `C_a` is an indicator reduction; it does not remove its rate
and regularity conditions. The multi-arm missing-outcome extension follows the armwise
Díaz and van der Laan algorithm and inherits its conditions for each arm. Finite
stacking, linear contrasts and smooth delta-method transforms do not create new
unconditional guarantees.

## Concrete defects and fixes

### Composite initial clipping

`correction_check` called `targeting_inputs`, which bounded the original factors and
removed them before checking whether the composite was outside its bounds. The
already-bounded product could not expose initial factor clipping. A raw treatment
probability of 0.005 with floor 0.01 and observation probability 0.5 becomes composite
0.005. That lies above composite floor 0.0001 although the denominator doubled.

`composite_clipping_mask` now unions the original factors' clipping masks per row and
arm. It calls `Propensity.truncate`, preserving its binary complement convention and
near-simplex tolerance. The old noncomposite initial-clipping readers now call that
same method. This also removes false clipping flags under asymmetric binary bounds.
Each cell counts once when several factors clip.

### Stratified correction identities

The targeting state stores normalized block scores `Pn[I_s H residual / p_s]`.
The diagnostic compared `score[column]` with `Pn[H residual]`. They are different
quantities on a nonzero witness. The marginal score is `sum_s p_s score_s`.

`_marginal_scores` reconstructs that weighted marginal score before checking its
identity with the reported correction. Outcome/reduction scores use stratum-major
layout. The shared binary mechanism uses the same layout. Armwise mechanisms and
the missing-outcome observation mechanism use arm-major layout. The helper takes
that distinction explicitly. Unstratified scores retain their values.

The new `test_drtmle_review_diagnostics.py` uses live one-round scores where they
remain nonzero. Saturated both-guard cases use hypothetical QR perturbations on the
original fitted treatment, mechanism, predictions and strata. The test independently
records equation (9)'s scores from `(QR_a/g_a)(C_a-g_a)` and each stratum multiplier;
QR does not enter the outcome correction equation. Every existing
complete/randomized/composite route and guard checks the reconstructed identities.
Nine cases also meet an explicit old-index mutation control. The `guard=("g",)`
fixture's live term is unsolved, so its refusal to judge that term is preserved.
These are diagnostic-reader witnesses, not fitted estimates of perturbed laws.
Other controls use independent weighted arithmetic for both layouts, raw-factor clipping, and asymmetric
near-simplex binary clipping controls. Root-run validation supplies the final result.
