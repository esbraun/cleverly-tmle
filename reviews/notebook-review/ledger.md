# Notebook review ledger

Consolidated from `findings/<stem>.md` (reviewer) and `verification/<stem>.md` (verifier), commit
`5f33902b`. One table per notebook, one row per finding ID. Rows whose ID ends `-N<k>` or `-NF<k>`
are verifier findings (verdict `NEW`). Where the verifier changed a severity, the row gives the
verifier's value and records the reviewer's in brackets. Where the verifier corrected a fix, the
row gives the corrected fix and says so.

## Orchestrator decisions

1. **No refusal showcases.** The user ruled that the examples are end-to-end examples that work.
   They must not showcase refusals. `refusal-inventory.md` lists each refusal cell and its
   replacement. A fix whose substance is "explain the refusal better" is marked
   **superseded by refusal removal**. A fix that is only partly about a refusal is marked
   **partly superseded**, and the part that survives is stated.
2. **PT-N1 is confirmed in closed form.** The `nonlinear_dgp` propensity logit is
   0.6 W1 - 0.4 W2^2 + 0.5 W2 W3 + 0.3 1(W4 > 0). For fixed W2, E[1/g | W2] >= c exp(0.525 W2^2),
   and against the N(0, 1) density the integrand grows as exp(0.025 W2^2). So E[1/g] is infinite.
   The ATE efficiency bound and nu_0^2 of the `navigation_data` law are infinite. Any "true nu^2"
   quoted from Monte Carlo (7.77 in PT/CF, 8.22 or 8.23 in DR) is a divergent integral. Fixes in
   this ledger that cite those values must not reuse them.
